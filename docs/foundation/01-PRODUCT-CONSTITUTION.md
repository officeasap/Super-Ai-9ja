# 01 — Product Constitution

## Product boundary
SUPER-AI-9JA is a Nigeria-first product with its own repository, environment
variables, backend configuration, deployment configuration, and release
history. SUPER-AI Kenya is a reference implementation, not a live dependency
or a target to overwrite.

## Product principles
1. Teach usable AI skills, not merely expose AI terminology.
2. Make English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa, and Igbo first-class.
3. Provide structured courses, clear learning objectives, lessons, practice,
   assessment, progress evidence, and appropriate instructor oversight.
4. Make the experience responsive, accessible, and usable on mobile devices.
5. Ground educational answers in approved learning resources where available.
6. Respect learner privacy, protect accounts, and enforce server-side access.
7. Record provenance and licensing for imported curriculum and media.
8. Keep free and paid feature boundaries explicit if monetization is adopted.
9. Do not make unapproved assumptions about providers, prices, domains,
   hosting regions, payment rails, or legal compliance.

## Intended capability areas
- Public learning and product information
- Account registration, login, and learner dashboard
- Course catalogue and course/lesson pages
- Learning progress and assessment
- Course-grounded AI tutoring
- Approved video and learning-resource links
- Staff and instructor workflows, where authorized
- Optional payments, certificates, and premium features after decisions

## Definition of done
A feature is not complete merely because it renders. Its authorization,
loading/error/empty states, mobile behavior, language handling, accessibility,
tests, and production configuration must be checked as applicable.
