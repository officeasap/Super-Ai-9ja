# 05 — Curriculum and Tutor

## Learning contract
Each course should define its audience, prerequisites, measurable objectives,
language availability, source provenance, expected outcomes, and assessment
approach.

Each lesson should support, where appropriate:
- A clear objective and explanation
- Curated references or media with attribution
- Practice or reflection
- A check for understanding
- Progress evidence and next-step guidance

## Learning-system requirements
- Course and lesson ordering must be deterministic.
- Draft and unpublished content must not leak to unauthorized learners.
- Progress must be tied to the correct learner and course/lesson.
- Completion rules must be explicit and server-validated.
- Assessment attempts and results must be auditable.
- Instructor access must follow explicit permissions.
- Certificates, if adopted, require verifiable issuance and revocation rules.

## Tutor behavior
- Ground course-specific answers in approved lesson material where possible.
- Distinguish source-backed facts from general explanation and uncertainty.
- Never invent citations, lesson links, course completion, or credentials.
- Apply safe refusal and escalation behavior for harmful requests.
- Respect the requested response language without claiming perfect fluency.
- Limit input size, conversation history, retrieval scope, latency, and cost.
- Avoid exposing other learners' records or internal prompts/secrets.

## Curriculum source governance
Track source title, URL, owner, license, retrieval date, adaptation, attribution,
and review status. Do not republish material merely because it is accessible.
Exclude sources with unclear rights until reviewed.
